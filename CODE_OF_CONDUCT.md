# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Willowbrook exists to give kids and families a safe, welcoming, private place to play together. We expect our open-source community to reflect those same values.

We pledge to make participation in Willowbrook a harassment-free, welcoming experience for everyone—regardless of age, background, experience level, identity, or whether you contribute code, art, testing, documentation, or ideas.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive community:

- Being patient, kind, and constructive—especially with first-time contributors, student artists, and parents asking setup questions.
- Focusing feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respecting different skill sets (code, 3D art, audio, UX, documentation, and testing are all valued equally).
- Keeping discussions, screenshots, and contributed content family-friendly and appropriate for a kids' game.
- Protecting family privacy at all times.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Sharing sexually explicit, violent, hateful, or otherwise inappropriate content or assets.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- **Sharing children's personal information**—never post children's names, photos, account details, chat logs, save files, credentials, or private family server URLs in issues, pull requests, discussions, or screenshots.
- Submitting copyrighted or non-redistributable assets without proper licensing and attribution.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies across all Willowbrook project spaces—including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any official community channels—as well as when representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting & Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair, appropriate action in response to any behavior that violates them (such as removing content, issuing a warning, or temporarily/permanently banning a participant from project spaces).

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior—or spot accidentally posted private family information that needs immediate removal—please report it privately to the maintainers at **[criticalbitflip@willowbrookgame.com](mailto:criticalbitflip@willowbrookgame.com)**.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
