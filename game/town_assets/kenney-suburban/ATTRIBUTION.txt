Two scenic building models, one tree, planter and fence. Cloned/scaled and rendered with lightweight Lambert materials; no geometry modification.
Three.js GLTFLoader / BufferGeometryUtils are from installed three0.182.0, MIT; local import paths adjusted. Three.js existing license applies.

v0.12: A/H meshes reused on playable buildings, nonuniform fitted scale, shader-cut door openings, original interiors retained.
