Willowbrook Scuba starter mesh provenance
========================================

The individual GLBs in this folder are isolated groups from two starter GLBs
downloaded by the maintainer. Source material names and asset/node identities
are retained in ASSET-MANIFEST.json and in each GLB asset.extras record.

Diving and Underwater Salvage, by 3DAssets.dev, licensed CC0 1.0 Universal
https://3dassets.dev/packs/diving-and-underwater-salvage
Publisher starter asset 28464: Aircraft Salvage Worksite (model (5).glb locally).

Coral Reef and Underwater Scenery, by 3DAssets.dev, licensed CC0 1.0 Universal
https://3dassets.dev/packs/underwater-reef-worlds
Publisher starter asset 7075: Reef wall with swim-through (model (6).glb locally).

License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Preparation: Removed starter placement at each asset root. Decoded existing
quantization, baked authored per-mesh transforms into the original vertex
data, removed vertices unused by the selected triangles, and batched surfaces
by their unchanged original materials. Separated the two authored fins along
their disconnected negative/positive X groups so they can move with each
foot; no triangles were created. The source steel WebP texture was resampled
from 512 to 256 and embedded as PNG for portable core glTF import. All packaged
GLBs are core glTF 2.0 only, without any external references.
