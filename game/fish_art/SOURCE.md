Quaternius Animated Cute Fish Pack
Official source: https://quaternius.com/packs/cutefish.html
License: CC0 (official pack page; source/license verified during original beach work).
Original publisher download: fish.zip, unchanged and not included.
All 35 fish FBX files converted with Blender 5.0.1 to binary glTF with original animations retained. Imported material alpha normalized to 1 to preserve opaque cartoon colors. No rod, lure, worm, dock or boat is included in this fish catalog.
Catalog names and star rewards are Willowbrook game design; values do not represent real-world fish prices or conservation status. Higher rewards use strictly lower random-selection weights.
