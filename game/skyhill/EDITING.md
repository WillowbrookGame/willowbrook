# Skyhill Park: first flight
Open game/skyhill/skyhill.tscn in Godot and run the current scene (F6) for a standalone flight with the default avatar. Run the project (F5), then visit the trailhead east of Town's playground for the round trip with the current player's avatar. Signed-in browser players and native LAN peers on the same build can see one another on the overlook and in flight. Standalone F6 stays offline. Edit `glider_view.tscn` to change the glider and rider placement for both local and remote views.

- Press E or choose Launch. The glider moves forward on its own. A/D or Left/Right bank it; W or Up lowers the nose to descend, and S or Down raises it to climb. On touch, drag the same stick left/right to bank, up to descend, or down to climb. Keep straight for the easy approach, or steer right toward the Waterfall lift marker and blue wind ribbons. Climbing with the controls is limited. For longer flights, follow the lookout cues from Waterfall lift to East ridge lift, West ridge lift, then back to the waterfall. Circle in the ridge lift before crossing the valley; descend toward the waterfall so you meet it below its top. Take the meadow any time, and level out before touching down.
- A level meadow landing eases into its approach and coasts to a stop. Holding a steep dive or hard bank into touchdown, or hitting solid terrain, causes a brief tumble and sends you back to the deck without a penalty. Ease off the controls before touchdown. Press E or choose the on-screen button to return early. Leaving the course without hitting anything still returns you directly.
- In the editor, Course/Launch sets the start and facing. Course/WaterfallLift, Course/RidgeLift and Course/WestLift are editable updraft markers; each LiftBounds sets its footprint and height. The ridge fields taper at the top and do not stack. Move the marker and its ribbon group together for an accurate editor preview. Course/FlightBounds sets the horizontal recovery limit; keep it inside the network coordinate range (X/Z ±240), and the top of the highest field below the Skyhill presence ceiling (Y=88). Course/MeadowCenter and LandingBounds set the touchdown target; keep them aligned with Scenery/LandingMeadow. Move any rail, tree, or prop in Scenery. Move solid course geometry by its StaticBody3D so its collision comes with it.
- Scenery uses instanced GLB assets (original Skyhill terrain, deck, waterfall, and flight markers, plus the selected Kenney props). Select each instance or its parent in Godot to move it. The valley terrain asset imports its own triangle collision from the same geometry, so the rolling shoulders stay solid when moved. The gray cliffs and mossy scenic ridge also import collision with their meshes. Move individual cliff sections or the group root. The landing surface and deck keep separate simple collision shapes; preserve their fit when swapping art. Move Creek, Woodland, and BanksAndWildflowers independently in the scene. Original models can also be opened in Blender or rebuilt with `python game/skyhill/art/build_skyhill_art.py`.
- The lift sound is an original, local cue. Visible ribbons and the climb carry the mechanic with sound off. Positional waterfall ambience is deferred until browser and device testing.
- Optional flight goals are local to each pilot. The teal Meadow rings follow the straight beginner path; the gold Ridge rings go from the waterfall through the East ridge and across to the West ridge. Fly through the openings in each trail's order; there is no timer, shared score, or penalty for skipping them. The rings are editable meshes in `flight_goals.tscn`: move or rotate each node, and use uniform scale to resize its opening. Child order is flight order.
- The meadow bullseye is `Course/MeadowCenter/LandingTarget`. Keep it on the meadow; it has no collision. A clean touchdown within 4 m of its center earns Bullseye, within 9 m earns Great, and anywhere else in the meadow earns Nice. Moving after touchdown doesn't change the grade.
- Each route windsock instances `windsock.tscn`. Move the whole instance so its solid post and cap follow the visual; the fabric has no collision.
- The soft glider shadow samples the existing terrain layer underneath, not the windsock layer. It is a visual cue only and uses a flat transparent mesh for the browser renderer.
- Switching browser tabs or unfocusing the game pauses a flight, a landing, or a crash. Use Resume or E after returning, then press or touch the controls again. For browser QA, switch away mid-turn with a control held and verify that the glider stays put and the old input does not carry over. Reload the scene once and repeat.
- In `game/town.tscn`, the `SkyhillTrailhead` instance owns its sign, interaction marker and Town return marker. Move the instance together, or edit those markers in `town_trailhead.tscn`; keep each marker on walkable ground and leave clear space around the return marker.
- Open `overlook.tscn` to edit the bench, picnic table, on-foot arrival spot, and glider interaction marker. Move the entire PicnicTable or Bench node to carry its art, collider and seat markers together. A seat marker sits on the cushion or plank and its +Z faces the seated avatar’s forward direction. Its Approach marker stays on the deck, clear of furniture collision. Keep the walkway collider and edge guards aligned with the deck. On-foot colliders are on a separate physics layer from the flight course.
- Walk with WASD, arrow keys or the touch stick after arriving from Town. Press E or tap Sit beside either side of the picnic table or in front of the bench. Stand up returns you to the same spot you walked up from. Launch is available only beside the glider. Ride back up after landing, or recover from a crash, to return to the overlook on foot. Use Back to Town (button or R) there. The small `town_link.gd` module passes the avatar and return intent without changing saves or requiring a new network zone.
