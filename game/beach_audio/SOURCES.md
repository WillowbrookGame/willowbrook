- `cast_whoosh.wav`: el_boss, [Casting Fishing Rod for Game Fishing SFX](https://freesound.org/people/el_boss/sounds/853287/). First 0.48 seconds of `853287__el_boss__casting-fishing-rod-for-game-fishing-sfx.wav`, with an 80 ms tail fade, to finish before the bobber lands.
- `splash_12.ogg`: rubberduck, [40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx). Original `splash_12.ogg` from the pack.

- `catch_splash.wav`: paulprit, [Fish Jumping Splash 2](https://freesound.org/people/paulprit/sounds/507091/). Trimmed 1.044–1.566 seconds around the first main water-breaking splash; 6 ms fade-in and 115 ms tail fade. Converted from float to 16-bit stereo PCM.
- `fish_nibble.wav`: damarlo, [Waterdrop falling on water surface](https://freesound.org/people/damarlo/sounds/736528/). Temporary, replaceable nibble cue. Trimmed to a single drop at 1.513–1.734 seconds, with an 8 ms fade-in and 70 ms tail fade; converted from 24-bit to 16-bit stereo PCM.
